Project Summary

Built for 2.008 (Design and Manufacturing II), these Lego-themed yoyo's were designed and built alongside five other students. Click Here to see the full project report, outlining all of the analysis and design choices that lead to the final yo-yos.

Class Requirements: Design a yo-yo for mass manufacturing by injection molding and thermal forming, submit 50 yo-yo's with a proper pressfit.

Team Requirements: Design a Lego-Themed yo-yo where a pair of yo-yo's can fit together, reminiscent of the real life lego!
